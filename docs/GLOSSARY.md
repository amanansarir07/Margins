# Margins Glossary

- **Attitude control**: Hardware that points the spacecraft and instruments accurately.
- **Budget**: The mission's cost limit, measured in millions of dollars ($M).
- **Comms / communications**: The radio system used to send science data home.
- **Data returned fraction**: The share of generated instrument data that the spacecraft can downlink.
- **Dry mass**: Component mass before propellant is added.
- **Event**: A seeded mission disruption, such as a flare, dust storm, or instrument fault.
- **Game model**: A simplified value created for gameplay rather than a verified NASA fact.
- **Margin**: The limit minus the amount used. A positive margin leaves room; a negative margin fails the check.
- **NASA-based**: A number or fact linked to a recorded NASA source.
- **Orbit**: The selected flyby, high, or low observation path around a destination.
- **Propellant**: Fuel mass estimated from dry mass, destination, and orbit.
- **Reliability**: A mission health value reduced by damaging events.
- **Science points**: The model's measure of how well the instruments meet the mission goal.
- **Source note**: The explanation or citation attached to every data record.
